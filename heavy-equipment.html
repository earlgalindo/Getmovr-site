Heavy Equipment

What we transport

Oversized loads go wrong when someone guesses at the weight or the doorway. We measure and plan the lift first, then quote — which is why these jobs are priced individually rather than off a rate card.

Machinery and shop equipment

Industrial and workshop machinery moved between sites, into new premises, or out of a closing facility.

Industrial and oversized appliances

Commercial kitchen equipment, safes, large format printers and similar items that are past what a standard moving crew is equipped for.

Job site equipment moves

Equipment relocated between active job sites, scheduled around your build so the crew is not left waiting on a delivery.

Rigging and load security

Items are strapped, braced and secured for transport, and access at both ends is confirmed before pickup is scheduled.

FAQ

Heavy Equipment questions

How large an item can you move?

It depends on weight, footprint and the access at both ends. Send the make and model or rough dimensions and weight with your quote request and we will tell you plainly whether it is within what we handle.

What information do you need to quote?

Item dimensions and approximate weight, plus the access at both the pickup and delivery ends — floor level, door widths, stairs or ramps, and where a truck can park.

Are heavy equipment moves insured?

Yes. Liability insurance covers these jobs the same as any other, and load security is part of the job rather than an add-on.

How much notice do you need?

More than a standard move — the access assessment happens before we schedule. A few days notice is usually enough, but call as early as you can for tight timelines.

Can you move equipment between active job sites?

Yes, and we schedule around your build. Tell us the window you need it in and we will confirm whether we can hit it before you commit.
