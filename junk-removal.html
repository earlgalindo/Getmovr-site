What we haul away

If you can point at it, we can usually take it. We load it, sweep up after ourselves, and sort out what can be donated or recycled instead of landfilled.

Furniture and appliances

Couches, mattresses, dressers, fridges, washers and dryers. We do the lifting and carrying — nothing needs to be moved to the curb beforehand.

Renovation and construction debris

Drywall, flooring, cabinetry, fixtures and general contractor debris cleared from active job sites or finished renovations.

Estate and garage cleanouts

Whole-property cleanouts handled discreetly and at your pace, including basements, garages and storage units.

Recycling and donation

Usable furniture and appliances go to donation where possible, and recyclable material is separated out rather than sent straight to landfill.

Pricing

How junk removal is priced

Volume-based pricing is the fairest model for hauling, and it is the one we use.

Volume

Space used in the truck

You are quoted on the fraction of the truck your items fill. A heavy appliance and a bulky-but-light couch are priced the same if they take the same space.

Never by weight

Weight is not a factor

Weight-based pricing punishes you for having dense items. We do not use it, so a load of tile does not cost more than a load of pillows of the same size.

Labour included

No curbside requirement

Carrying items out of a basement or upstairs bedroom is included. You do not need to drag anything to the curb before we arrive.

FAQ

Junk Removal questions

What items can't you take?

We're unable to take hazardous materials — paint, solvents, chemicals, propane tanks, asbestos — and certain electronics restricted under local disposal rules. Ask us and we'll point you to the right local disposal option.

Do you offer same-day junk removal?

In most of Toronto and Mississauga, yes. Same-day depends on how the day is already booked, so call early for the best chance of a same-day slot.

How exactly is volume measured?

By the fraction of the truck bed your items occupy once loaded. We give you the price based on the estimated load before we start, so there is no surprise at the end.

Do I need to be home for the pickup?

Not necessarily. As long as we can access the items and someone is reachable by phone to confirm what is going, we can complete most pickups without you on site.

Do you clean up afterward?

Yes. We sweep the area once the load is out, so the space is left clear rather than covered in debris.
