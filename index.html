FAQ

Common questions

How far in advance should I book my move?

We recommend 1–2 weeks ahead for weekday moves, and 3–4 weeks ahead for weekends during peak season (May–September). We'll always do our best to accommodate shorter notice.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Every job is covered by liability insurance, and our crew follows standard safe-handling and transport practices.

How is pricing determined?

Moving jobs are quoted flat-rate based on volume and distance after a quick call or form submission. Junk removal is priced by the volume your items take up in the truck, not by weight. Heavy equipment jobs are quoted individually based on access and machinery needed.

What items can't you take for junk removal?

We're unable to take hazardous materials (paint, chemicals, propane tanks) or certain electronics under local disposal rules. Ask us and we'll point you to the right local disposal option.

Do I need to be on-site for the whole move?

We ask that someone authorized to make decisions be reachable by phone throughout, but you don't need to be present the entire time if access has been arranged in advance.

What happens if something gets damaged?

Our crew takes care to protect your belongings during every job. In the rare case something is damaged, it's handled through our insurance — we'll walk you through the process directly.
