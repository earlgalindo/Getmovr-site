What a Movr move includes

Most moving quotes fall apart on move day because they were priced by the hour. We quote by volume and distance instead, so the number you agree to is the number you pay.

Homes and apartments

Studio through multi-bedroom houses, including walk-ups and high-rise buildings across the GTA. We plan for stairs, tight hallways and narrow elevators before the truck arrives.

Offices and commercial

FAQ

Moving questions

How far in advance should I book my move?

We recommend 1–2 weeks ahead for weekday moves, and 3–4 weeks ahead for weekends during peak season (May–September). Shorter notice is often possible — call and ask.

Do you provide boxes and packing materials?

Yes. We can supply boxes, tape and protective wrap, and provide packing or unpacking help as part of the quote. Tell us on the quote request and it is included in the flat rate.

Can you handle stairs, walk-ups and elevator buildings?

Yes. Tell us the floor and whether there is an elevator when you request your quote. We factor stairs and long carries into the price up front rather than adding a surcharge on move day.

Do you disassemble and reassemble furniture?

Yes — beds, tables, shelving and similar items are taken apart before loading and put back together at the destination.

Do you move long distance or only within the GTA?

Both. Local moves within Toronto and Mississauga are our core work, and we take longer hauls out of region with the distance priced into the flat rate.
